Features and what's new

Sugar Daddy is a friendly iPhone companion for Dexcom users, made by a fellow diabetic for our community. It shows the glucose number you already follow in places that are quick to reach, and lets you make it look like you.

Dexcom stays your safety net. Sugar Daddy is not a medical device and does not replace your Dexcom app, receiver or medical advice. Keep your Dexcom app and its Urgent Low and Low alerts turned on, and check your Dexcom app or do a fingerstick when something doesn't look right. Never make treatment decisions based on Sugar Daddy. Sugar Daddy is not affiliated with Dexcom, Inc.
Good to know: iPhone only, iOS 17.0 or newer. There is no Apple Watch app. You sign in with your Dexcom account, with Share turned on in Dexcom's own app.

What Sugar Daddy does

  • Dexcom sign-in. Sign in with the Dexcom account of the person wearing the sensor. You can look around the app first and sign in whenever you are ready.
  • Home. Your glucose number with its trend arrow and a clear graph with 1, 3, 6 and 12 hour views, plus a little pep-talk line.
  • Ask Siri. Ask Siri for your glucose, which way it is heading, what it was an hour ago, or how long you have been in range. A how-to screen in Settings lists the phrases, and a one-time banner on Home points you to it.
  • Widgets. Put your number and a little graph on your Home Screen or Lock Screen. Each widget can follow the app's look or have its own.
  • Live Activity and Dynamic Island. Keep your number on the Lock Screen when you want it close.
  • Themes. Dozens of colors, fonts and looks, with a theme search and a "Surprise me" button if you can't decide.
  • Alert profiles. Everyday, Sleep, Quiet Day and Workout. Each profile has its own alerts, levels and sounds, and the High and Low lines on your graph follow the profile that is active at that moment. Sleep takes over on a schedule (10 pm to 7 am by default).
  • Sweet History. A calendar of your past days, with time in range, plus quick tags and notes.
  • Emergency contact and Get Help. After you sign in to Dexcom, set up an emergency contact with a short walkthrough. Then Get Help opens a text to that contact with your latest reading. You review it and press Send. It is manual only and never calls anyone.
  • Sharing and export. Send your numbers to someone in a text, and export your history as a CSV file.

About alerts: Sugar Daddy alerts depend on iOS Background App Refresh and can be delayed or missed. Low alerts are Time Sensitive, not Critical Alerts. They are a convenience on top of your Dexcom alerts, never a replacement.

About Pro: optional extras may come later. Everything listed above, including Siri, widgets, alerts, history and sharing, does not depend on it.

What's new in 0.8.6 (in testing)

Version 0.8.6 (build 13) is being tested right now, and things can still change before release. TestFlight is coming soon.

  • Siri how-to. A new "Siri: how to" screen lists every phrase, and a one-time "Try asking Siri about your sugar" banner on the graph opens it.
  • Settings, tidied up. Settings are reordered, with Siri right under Account. A new Help section covers Siri, connecting Dexcom, late or missing readings, and contacting support.
  • Faster start. Home opens right away with your last known reading and a dimmed graph, then updates.
  • Switching Dexcom accounts is safer. A "Switch account?" prompt appears first, with an "Export CSV first" button. One person's history no longer mixes with another's.
  • Safer saving. Saving your history, signing out and Delete All My Data are more careful.
  • Password stays private. The Connect screen shows "Password saved on this iPhone" and never reveals the password.
  • Clearer sign-in wording. The Connect screen says plainly to sign in with the Dexcom account of the person wearing the sensor.
  • Simpler sharing. The "Invite someone" screen is gone. "Share Sugar Daddy" stays.
  • Send diagnostics. Send diagnostics opens an email with a short technical log attached (no readings, logins, locations or contacts). Nothing is sent until you press Send. Delete All My Data also removes the diagnostics log.

Screenshots on this site carry the caption "Application in testing, appearance subject to change".

Coming later

Support for other CGMs, such as Libre, is planned and under research for a later update. There is no date yet. For now Sugar Daddy works with Dexcom through Dexcom Share.
