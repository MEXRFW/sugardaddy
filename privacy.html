Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

The short version

Sugar Daddy works on your device only. Tech Mecks collects no data and operates no servers. Anything the App saves stays on your iPhone, under your control. The App contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking code. We cannot see your readings, your login, or anything else you do in the App.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Dexcom Share username and password. Kept only in this device's iOS Keychain, and shared only with the App's own widget, Siri shortcuts, and Live Activity on this device. They are never sent anywhere except to Dexcom to sign in.
  • Your settings and alert profiles. For example: units, region, thresholds, schedules, Bedtime hours, Bedside mode options, and which sounds you chose.
  • Emergency contacts you choose. Name and phone number only.
  • Sounds you import.
  • Your glucose history and tags. Readings the App fetches from Dexcom, plus tags and notes you add (for example "🍕 Meal: pizza"), are saved only on this iPhone, in the App's private storage, so you can see Sweet History and time in range. They are never uploaded or sent to us. You choose how long they're kept (24 hours by default; 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, 1 year, or Forever) in Settings → Your Data, and older entries are deleted automatically.
  • Live Activity schedules. The days and times you choose for reminders.
  • Diagnostics log. A short technical log is kept only on your iPhone. It lists error messages and results (for example a failed Dexcom refresh with its status code) and a few technical details: the app version, iOS version, iPhone model identifier, whether Background App Refresh and Low Power Mode are on, and the theme in use. It never includes your readings, logins, locations or contacts. It stays on your device unless you choose Settings → Send diagnostics, which opens an email you review and send yourself; only then do these details leave your iPhone. It is deleted with Delete All My Data.
  • Sample data (demo mode). The App can show a demo mode with simulated glucose readings, history and events so the App can be explored without a Dexcom account. Sample data is made up on your iPhone, is not real health data, is kept apart from any real data, and is never uploaded or sent anywhere. Quitting demo mode (Settings) or Delete All My Data removes it.

What is never saved

  • Readings off your device. Your readings are saved only on this iPhone and never leave it, except when you choose to share them (for example a text, a widget picture, or a CSV export). Siri, widgets, and alerts always fetch fresh from Dexcom and show "No data" if that fails.
  • Location. Used for Get Help texts: read when you tap Get Help, then put into the text message you choose to send. If you tap Near me in the Themes tab, Sugar Daddy reads your approximate location (about 3 km) on this iPhone to show your nearest city's themes. Sugar Daddy asks for location for this only after you tap Near me and Continue. Location is never stored, tracked in the background, or sent anywhere else.
  • Analytics. There are none. No usage tracking, advertising IDs, or crash reporting.

Connections to Dexcom

Your device signs in to Dexcom's Share servers directly with your credentials, and gets your readings straight from them. Dexcom handles that data under its own privacy policy.

Messages you send

"Share my numbers" and "Get Help" open Apple's Messages or share sheet with a prefilled message. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send.

Purchases

If optional extras are offered in the future, any purchase would be processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment or personal details.

Your controls

  • Settings → Sign Out of Dexcom deletes your Dexcom login from this device.
  • Settings → Delete All My Data erases everything the App keeps on this device, including saved readings and tags, and resets the health agreement.
  • Deleting the App removes its data too. Because of how iOS works, Keychain items can remain after deletion; use Delete All My Data first to be sure.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we will update the date above.
