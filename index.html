FAQ

How do I set up Dexcom Share?

Sugar Daddy reads the same feed your followers see. In the Dexcom G7 app, open Connections and turn on Share (invite at least one follower). Then open Sugar Daddy, choose your region, and sign in with your Dexcom username (email or phone number) and password. Your login is kept only in this iPhone's Keychain.

How do I ask Siri?

Say "Hey Siri, get my sugar in Sugar Daddy". You can also ask whether you're going up or down, what your sugar was an hour ago, or how long you've been in range. The phrases also appear in the Shortcuts app.

How do I add a widget?

On the Home Screen, touch and hold an empty area, tap Edit → Add Widget, and choose Sugar Daddy. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then tap the widget area. Widgets follow the app's theme ("Match app") by default; open Edit Widget to give one widget its own theme.

Why is my reading late or showing "No data"?

Dexcom Share can be delayed or unavailable, and iOS decides how often widgets refresh. Check that Share is on in your Dexcom app and that your phone is online. Always confirm with your Dexcom app or a fingerstick.

Does Sugar Daddy collect my data?

No. There are no servers, analytics or tracking. Your readings and settings stay on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions, choose Sugar Daddy Pro and tap Cancel. Apple handles billing and refunds.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete All My Data. This erases your Dexcom login, saved readings, tags and settings from this iPhone.
